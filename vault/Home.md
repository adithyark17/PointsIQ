# PointsIQ Knowledge Graph

Every answer is a walk through this map. Start anywhere:

## The rewards core

- **[[HDFC Infinia]]** — the benchmark Indian premium card
- **[[Axis EDGE Miles]]** — the most transfer-connected currency
- **[[American Express Membership Rewards India]]**
- Browse: `cards/` · `currencies/` · `banks/` · `portals/`

## Moving points

- Transfer routes live in `transfers/` — each note is a **named edge** with ratio,
  transfer time and a verified date. Open any currency note for its live partner table.
- Award pricing starters: **[[KrisFlyer Saver Chart]]**, **[[British Airways Avios Distance Chart]]**,
  **[[Air India Maharaja Club Chart]]** — see `award-charts/`.

## The travel side

- Frequent flyer programs: `programs/air/` — e.g. **[[Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer]]**, **[[Aeroplan]]**
- Hotel programs: `programs/hotel/` — [[Marriott Bonvoy]] · [[Accor ALL]] · [[IHG One Rewards]] ·
  [[Hilton Honors]] · [[World of Hyatt]] · [[Wyndham Rewards]] · [[ITC Club]] · [[Taj NeuPass]] ·
  [[Radisson Rewards]] · [[Choice Privileges]]
- **[[Star Alliance]] · [[oneworld]] · [[SkyTeam]]**
- Airports: `airports/<country>/` — ~6,400 nodes keyed by IATA code, e.g. **[[DEL]]**, **[[BOM]]**, **[[SIN]]**

## Stopover programs

- Break one award into two trips: `stopovers/` — free hotels at [[Turkish Airlines Stopover|IST]],
  [[Discover Qatar Stopover|DOH ($14 hotels)]], [[Saudia Stopover Program|JED]]; free routing up to
  10 days via [[TAP Portugal Stopover]] and [[Icelandair Stopover]]; see **[[Stopover]]** for the concept.

## Spend categories

- MCC categories in `mcc/` — e.g. **[[Dining]]**, **[[Fuel]]**, **[[Rent]]** — link each category
  to the cards that accelerate or exclude it.

## Learn the game

- Concepts & glossary: `concepts/` — **[[Cents Per Point]]**, **[[Transfer Bonus]]**,
  **[[Devaluation]]**, **[[Fuel Surcharge (YQ)]]**, **[[Sweet Spot]]**

## Graph view tips

- The default graph filter hides medium airports (`-tag:#airport/medium`). Clear it to see
  the full 6K-airport field cluster around country hubs.
- Color legend: cards (red) · currencies (orange) · transfers (yellow) · air programs (blue) ·
  hotel programs (purple) · airlines (cyan) · banks (pink) · MCC (green) · geo (teal) · airports (gray).
